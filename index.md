## Research
* I started a master's degree in 2025 at the Unversity of Michigan, working on global climate models.
* I'm interested in large-eddy simulation of atmospheric thermals to help glider pilots, and planetary atmosphere modeling of planets like Uranus or Mars.
## Work
* Previously, I worked in the space industry at Firefly Aerospace as a flight software engineer, where I controlled rockets, orbital transfer vehicles and even landed code on the surface of the moon.
* I'm a frequent contributor to the [FPrime](https://github.com/nasa/fprime) flight software framework, developed by the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory.
* I develop the [Fpy](https://github.com/fprime-community/fpy) spacecraft sequencing language for FPrime.
